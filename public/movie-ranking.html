The best movies according to Alazne

My top 3 movies of all time

How To Train Your Dragon

The whole saga is a masterpiece. Beautiful animation, it made me cry like a baby (especially the 2nd one). Plus, it has dragons! How cool is that?

Rental Family

Both Brendan Fraser and Hikari deliver a fantastic performance. A movie about building genuine relationships with both comedy and drama, it leaves you all warm inside.

Project Hail Mary

Funny, beautiful, emotive, shows you the wonders of space. I read the book before seeing the movie, and when I found out Ryan Gosling played the main character I thought it was a great casting choice (Ryland Grace has Ken vibes for sure).
